# Roadmap

## Proposed Features

Collected ideas for future development. Not actively planned or prioritized.

1. **Author Profiles** -- view all archived comments by any author, see their total comment score and activity over time, and optionally hide their comments from thread views and search results.

2. **Multi-Term Search** -- search for multiple terms at once using AND logic, for example finding comments that mention both a player name and a specific topic in the same result.

3. **Markdown Rendering** -- render formatted text in comment bodies so bold, italics, links, and lists display properly instead of raw symbols.

4. **Thread Activity Badge** -- flag threads with significantly higher comment volume than usual with a visual badge, making it easy to spot days when discussion was especially active.

5. **Saved Searches** -- save frequently used search queries by name and rerun them with one tap from the search page.

6. **Player Watchlist and Roster** -- maintain a personal list of players you own or are tracking; filter search results to quickly surface only comments mentioning your players.

7. **Comment Edit Badges** -- show a visual indicator on comments that were deleted, removed, or edited after being archived, and allow filtering to find them across the archive.

8. **Player Mention Trends** -- track how often player names appear across all archived comments over a configurable time window, surfacing who the community is discussing most. Name matching has known limitations around shared last names and nicknames.

9. **Expanded Thread Sources** -- archive comments from a broader range of r/fantasybaseball posts beyond the daily Anything Goes threads, such as waiver wire, start/sit, and trade advice discussions.

10. **Search Autocomplete** -- show name suggestions as you type in the search bar, drawing from terms and players already present in the archive.

11. **Search Highlighting** -- highlight the matched search term inside each comment body on the results page so the relevant text stands out at a glance.

12. **Progressive Web App** -- add a service worker and web manifest so the app can be fully installed to your home screen and used offline with cached data.

13. **Activity Alerts** -- get notified when something notable happens in the archive, such as a sudden spike in mentions of a player or a thread crossing an unusual activity threshold. Delivery options could include push notifications or an in-app banner.
