# Roadmap

Tracks two kinds of ideas: planned features actually committed to a future release, and proposed features that are just collected ideas, not yet planned or prioritized.

## Planned Features

Nothing planned right now. All previously planned features have shipped.

## Proposed Features

Collected ideas for future development. Not actively planned or prioritized.

1. **Thread Activity Badge**: flag threads with significantly higher comment volume than usual with a visual badge, making it easy to spot days when discussion was especially active.

2. **Player Watchlist and Roster**: maintain a personal list of players you own or are tracking; filter search results to quickly surface only comments mentioning your players.

3. **Comment Edit Badges**: show a visual indicator on comments that were deleted, removed, or edited after being archived, and allow filtering to find them across the archive.

4. **Player Mention Trends**: track how often player names appear across all archived comments over a configurable time window, surfacing who the community is discussing most. Name matching has known limitations around shared last names and nicknames.

5. **Expanded Thread Sources**: archive comments from a broader range of r/fantasybaseball posts beyond the daily Anything Goes threads, such as waiver wire, start/sit, and trade advice discussions.

6. **Search Autocomplete**: show name suggestions as you type in the search bar, drawing from terms and players already present in the archive.

7. **Progressive Web App**: add a service worker and web manifest so the app can be fully installed to your home screen and used offline with cached data.

8. **Activity Alerts**: get notified when something notable happens in the archive, such as a sudden spike in mentions of a player or a thread crossing an unusual activity threshold. Delivery options could include push notifications or an in-app banner.

9. **Calendar View**: pick a start and end date from a calendar to scope search results to a specific date range of threads, instead of relying on the Days Back filter.
