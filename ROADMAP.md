# Roadmap

## Proposed Features

Collected ideas for future development. Not actively planned or prioritized.

1. **Author Profiles**: view all archived comments by any author, see their total comment score and activity over time, and optionally hide their comments from thread views and search results.

2. **Thread Activity Badge**: flag threads with significantly higher comment volume than usual with a visual badge, making it easy to spot days when discussion was especially active.

3. **Saved Searches**: save frequently used search queries by name and rerun them with one tap from the search page.

4. **Player Watchlist and Roster**: maintain a personal list of players you own or are tracking; filter search results to quickly surface only comments mentioning your players.

5. **Comment Edit Badges**: show a visual indicator on comments that were deleted, removed, or edited after being archived, and allow filtering to find them across the archive.

6. **Player Mention Trends**: track how often player names appear across all archived comments over a configurable time window, surfacing who the community is discussing most. Name matching has known limitations around shared last names and nicknames.

7. **Expanded Thread Sources**: archive comments from a broader range of r/fantasybaseball posts beyond the daily Anything Goes threads, such as waiver wire, start/sit, and trade advice discussions.

8. **Search Autocomplete**: show name suggestions as you type in the search bar, drawing from terms and players already present in the archive.

9. **Search Highlighting**: highlight the matched search term inside each comment body on the results page so the relevant text stands out at a glance.

10. **Progressive Web App**: add a service worker and web manifest so the app can be fully installed to your home screen and used offline with cached data.

11. **Activity Alerts**: get notified when something notable happens in the archive, such as a sudden spike in mentions of a player or a thread crossing an unusual activity threshold. Delivery options could include push notifications or an in-app banner.

12. **Calendar View**: pick a start and end date from a calendar to scope search results to a specific date range of threads, instead of relying on the Days Back filter.
